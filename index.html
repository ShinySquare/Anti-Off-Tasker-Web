What is the Anti-Off-Tasker?

The Anti-Off-Tasker is a application created by Luke that aims to stop oftasking during school by monitering the students' computers.

We have noticed that at least 75% of middle school children off-task or have off-tasked before

What our product does

Our Anti-Off-Tasker moniters the children's screens, warning the parent and in some models, automatically shuts off the off-tasking and checks if it really is off-tasking

We have 3 different models, each better than the previous
